Boulderdash

This game has you moving around a world represented by a grid of 32x32 tiles, of which 20x16 are visible on the screen around the player. 

There is a one tile thick border which is impassible

These tiles can be empty space, dirt, boulders, diamonds or an exit.

Mobile entities are diamond enemies, and explosive enemies

A explosion tile cycles through an explosion animation and then vanishes 

The player can move freely through empty space using the arrow keys.

When the player moves into a dirt space, the dirt is removed

When the player moves into a diamond space, the diamond is collected and removed

The player cannot move into a boulder space

The exit becomes available when enough diamonds have been collected

If there is nothing in the tile below a boulder it starts to fall.

If the boulder hits the player, the player dies in a 3x3 explosion

If the boulder hits a diamond enemy, the enemy dies in a 3x3 explosion of diamonds

If the boulder hits an explosive enemy, the enemy dies in a 3x3 explosion

Being caught in an explosion has the same effect as being hit by a boulder
